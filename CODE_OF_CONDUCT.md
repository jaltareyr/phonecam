# Community guidelines

PhoneCam welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.
Treat other people with respect and assume questions are asked in good faith.
Give specific, constructive technical feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and sharing someone's private information without permission are
not acceptable. These expectations apply in issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other spaces maintained for this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, close contributions,
or restrict participation to protect the community. They should explain moderation
decisions when doing so is safe and useful. Use GitHub's **Report content** feature
to flag abusive behavior; avoid repeating personal or sensitive details publicly.
Reports of security defects should use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
